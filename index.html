About me

I’m an operations and business support professional with an academic background in IT and practical experience in banking, finance and payment operations. My career interests lie at the intersection of business and technology, particularly in business operations, financial services and fintech.

I have completed a Higher Diploma in Computing & Software Engineering, which provided me with a foundation in databases, networking, systems analysis, business analytics and project management. Alongside this, I’m pursuing AAT qualifications to strengthen my knowledge of accounting, finance and business management.

I aim to further develop my expertise by pursuing a BSc in Business Information Systems. I see this as an opportunity to combine my existing IT knowledge with business strategy, operational management and technology-driven processes, building a stronger foundation for a long-term career in business and technology.

Through my experience in banking and payment operations, I’ve developed strengths in operational support, data accuracy, reporting, portfolio management and process coordination. I enjoy understanding how business processes, information systems and technology work together to improve efficiency, accuracy and overall business performance.

My goal is to grow within business operations, banking, financial services and fintech, applying both my business understanding and technical knowledge to support better processes and business outcomes.
